# AI Marketing Campaign Content Generator

## Project Overview
* The AI Marketing Campaign Content Generator will be an application that helps users create the beginning stages of a marketing-content draft. The user will provide information such as, product or service, target audience, goal, marketing channel, and desired tone. In turn, an open-source large language model will be used to create content based on the given information.

## Problem Statement
* Small businesses and entrepreneurs are focused on day to day tasks which might not leave them with enough time and effort to create marketing content for their products or services. Social landscape is constantly changing and it might be difficult for an individual to be able to change messages and content at a moments notice.  

## Why this project matters
* This app allows business owners and marketing creators with simple information to effectively start creating content and messaging that will in turn promote their products and services at their convenience. It will also help create one solid idea instead of multiple, not cohesive messaging. 

## Proposed Solution
* User will fill out form providing information, such as tone, target audience, marketing channel, campaign objective where the application will create a prompt, and that prompt will be sent to a open-source language model.
